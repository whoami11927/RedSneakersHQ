# Red Sneakers HQ visual direction

Owner direction: streetwear editorial, assertive and energetic, red used as an accent, with sneaker imagery and variants as the focus.

Reading: B2C sneaker retail for customers and store staff, with ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- Palette: warm paper and ink keep product photos legible; red identifies the store and marks primary actions. Light and dark values are checked for text contrast.
- Type: Barlow Condensed gives product headings a compact streetwear voice; Geist keeps forms, prices, and stock figures easy to scan.
- Store layout: a broad editorial introduction leads directly to filters and products because the main customer task is selecting a sneaker and size.
- Admin layout: product/variant forms and a compact inventory table put SKU, location, available quantity, and ledger adjustment controls together because staff make stock decisions from those fields.
- Spacing: product sections use larger breaks to establish editorial rhythm; filter controls and stock rows stay compact to preserve useful information density.
- Surfaces: product cards group the photo, name, price, and availability; admin data uses a horizontally scrollable table inside each product card on narrow screens.
- Motion: hover and focus states only, to clarify action without delaying shopping or stock work.
- Imagery: no product photography or logo asset is fabricated. Admins can upload product images to the policy-protected Supabase Storage bucket; missing images use a visible placeholder, and the wordmark remains text.
- Theme: light is the initial theme so product colors read against a neutral surface; a working toggle supports staff and customers who prefer dark surfaces.
